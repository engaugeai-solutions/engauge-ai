FAQ

Questions, answered

Will callers know they're talking to AI?

The voice is natural and trained on your services and tone, so most callers simply get answered and booked. We can have it introduce itself as a virtual assistant if you prefer.

What if a caller asks something it can't handle?

You set the rules. It can transfer the call to your team, take a detailed message, or text staff a summary so someone calls back.

Which processes can you automate?

Almost anything repetitive that moves between apps: lead handling, invoicing, payment reminders, reporting, onboarding, order updates and inbox triage. We'll show you the biggest time-savers on the free audit.

Do I need technical skills or new software?

No. We work with the tools you already use, build everything for you and manage it after launch.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. Forward your existing number to the agent all the time, only after hours, or only when your line is busy.
